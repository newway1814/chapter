Chapter 12: A Quiet Afternoon on the Porch

The rain started just as the tea went cold. There was a quietness to the street that felt almost heavy, a stillness that preceded the steady rhythm of drops against the wooden planks.

I watched the steam slowly disappear from the ceramic rim, tracing the delicate spiderweb cracks that had formed over years of use. It was an afternoon designed for forgetting, or perhaps, for remembering too clearly.

Every passing car sounded like a distant wave, temporarily breaking the silence before washing away completely. The porch swing creaked slightly in the growing breeze, a rhythmic companion to my thoughts.

I gathered the cold cup in both hands, not for warmth, but for grounding. Some days demand nothing more than bearing witness to the weather, and finding an odd comfort in the melancholy of an overcast sky.
